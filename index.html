Who we are

ParkTrace is made by SS Pixels. In this policy, “we” means SS Pixels and “the app” means ParkTrace on Android.

ParkTrace has no accounts and no servers of its own. We never receive your parking data, your location or your photos. The only services the app talks to are Google’s, as described below.

What the app stores on your phone

When you save a spot, ParkTrace keeps the following in private storage on your device, where other apps cannot read it:

  • Location — the coordinates of your parked car and how accurate the reading was.
  • Time — when you parked and when you cleared the spot.
  • Details you add — floor, zone, row, spot, pillar and notes.
  • Photos you take or choose — copied into the app’s own storage. ParkTrace does not read the rest of your photo library.
  • Settings — units, theme, vibration and whether you have Premium.
  • Find parking usage — how many free searches you have used today, so the daily limit works. This resets every day.

This information stays on your device until you delete it.

What leaves your phone

Google Maps Platform. When you are online, ParkTrace sends the coordinates of your parked car to Google to look up a place name and address, and to load map images on the Find My Car screen. Google handles this under its own privacy policy. If you are offline, nothing is sent and the app still works.

Find parking (Google Places). When you use Find parking, ParkTrace sends your current location to Google Places to get a list of parking places near you, and receives their names, positions and, for Premium, details such as opening hours, rating, phone and website. This happens only when you open Find parking or search again. Results are kept in memory for a few minutes and are not saved. Your location is not sent to us. Google handles these requests under its privacy policy.

Ads (Google AdMob). ParkTrace is free with ads. If you have not bought Premium, the app shows banner, full-screen and optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s Google settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. ParkTrace Premium removes all ads.

Google Play. If you buy ParkTrace Premium, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that Premium has been purchased.

Android backup. If backup is turned on in your phone’s settings, Android may include ParkTrace’s data in the backup it saves to your own Google account. This is controlled by your device, not by us, and you can turn it off in your phone’s backup settings.

Permissions and why we ask

  • Location — to record where you parked, to guide you back, and to find parking near you. ParkTrace uses your location only while the app is open. It does not track you in the background.
  • Notifications — to send a parking reminder, and only if you set one.
  • Camera — ParkTrace does not ask for camera permission. When you take a photo, it opens your phone’s own camera app.
  • Internet — for place names, maps, parking search, ads and Google Play purchases.
  • Advertising ID — used by Google AdMob to show ads to free users.
  • Run at startup — so a reminder you set still arrives after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration — for feedback when you save a spot or arrive at your car. You can turn this off in Settings.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t require an account or collect your name, email or phone number.
  • We don’t use our own analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We don’t sell or rent your data. The only advertising data is what Google AdMob collects directly, as described above.
  • We don’t track your location in the background or keep a record of your parking searches.

Deleting your data

You are in control of everything ParkTrace stores:

  • Delete a single spot from Parking history.
  • Delete all spots at once with Clear in Parking history.
  • Remove everything by uninstalling the app, or with Clear storage in your phone’s app settings.

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

ParkTrace is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how ParkTrace handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change reaches the app.
