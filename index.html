Who we are

ParkTrace is made by SS Pixels. In this policy, “we” means SS Pixels and “the app” means ParkTrace on Android.

ParkTrace has no accounts and no servers of its own. We never receive your parking data, your location or your photos. The only services the app talks to are Google’s, as described below.

What the app stores on your phone

When you save a spot, ParkTrace keeps the following in private app storage on your device, where other apps cannot read it:

  • Location: where your car is parked and how accurate the reading was.
  • Time: when you parked and when you cleared the spot.
  • Details you add: floor, pillar, zone, row, spot and notes.
  • Photos you take or choose: copied into the app’s own storage. ParkTrace does not read the rest of your photo library.
  • Reminders: the time you chose, so the alert can fire.
  • Settings: units, theme, vibration and whether you have Premium.
  • Find parking usage: how many free searches you have used today, so the daily limit works. It resets every day.

Two features read this data and nothing else:

  • Home-screen widget: shows whether a spot is saved and opens the app. It reads your active spot from the phone and sends nothing.
  • Past visits nearby (in Can’t find it): shows earlier spots you saved close to this one, from your own history on the phone.

All of this stays on your device until you delete it.

What leaves your phone

Google Maps Platform. When you are online, ParkTrace sends the coordinates of your parked car to Google to look up a place name and address, and to load maps. Google handles this under its own privacy policy. If you are offline, nothing is sent and the app still works.

Find parking (Google Places). When you use Find parking, ParkTrace sends your current location to Google Places to get parking places near you. It receives their names and positions and, when you open a spot, details such as opening hours, address and phone number. This happens only when you open Find parking or search again. Results are kept in memory for a few minutes and are not saved. Your location is never sent to us.

Ads (Google AdMob). ParkTrace is free with ads. Without Premium, the app shows banner, full-screen and optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s Google settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Premium removes all ads.

Google Play. If you buy Premium, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation that Premium is active.

Android backup. If backup is on in your phone’s settings, Android may include ParkTrace’s data in the backup it saves to your own Google account. Your device controls this, not us, and you can turn it off in your backup settings.

Permissions and why we ask

  • Location: to record where you parked, guide you back and find parking near you. Used only while the app is open. ParkTrace never tracks you in the background.
  • Notifications: to show a parking reminder, only if you set one.
  • Alarms & reminders: so your reminder arrives at the exact time you chose.
  • Run at startup: so a reminder you set still arrives after your phone restarts.
  • Camera: ParkTrace does not ask for camera permission. Taking a photo opens your phone’s own camera app.
  • Internet: for place names, maps, parking search, ads and Google Play purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob to show ads to free users.
  • Vibration: feedback when you save a spot or reach your car. You can turn it off in Settings.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t require an account or collect your name, email or phone number.
  • We don’t use our own analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We don’t sell or rent your data. The only advertising data is what Google AdMob collects directly, as described above.
  • We don’t track your location in the background or keep a record of your parking searches.

Deleting your data

You control everything ParkTrace stores:

  • Delete one spot from Parking history.
  • Delete all spots at once with the clear button in Parking history.
  • Remove everything by uninstalling the app, or with Clear storage in your phone’s app settings.

We hold none of your data, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

Age

ParkTrace is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change how ParkTrace handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change reaches the app.
